# Product Positioning Statement


Kivi is a voice-first assistant that remembers what a person said about their
work — not what a model guessed. It learns how someone deals with the world,
not how they talk to Kivi: who they work with, what they promised, how they
write to each person. That lets Hey Kivi answer, draft and track commitments
without being taught twice. A fact is kept only if the person said it about
themselves, it held up more than once, and traces back to a dictation. Kivi
learns nothing about anyone but the user, and never lets a guess pass as fact.
